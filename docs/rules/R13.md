# R13

## Rule

**R13.** One change per Spark: claim before the job exists, always release. Today: true for jobs made through
`claimed` and the page’s Deploy; a download-then-start job claims only after its download; `download`, `dig`,
`add-list`, `spark-action`, `workflow-run`, `validate-workflows` and `undo` jobs take no claim.

## Why

Two starts would share memory and port.
