# R13

## Rule

**R13.** One change per Spark: claim before the job exists, always release. Every job type is named here. Today: true
for jobs made through `claimed` and the page’s Deploy (`deploy`, `deploy-cluster`, `stop`, `restart`, `main-start`,
`main-stop`, `main-switch`, and the power operations `power-off` and `power-on`); a download-then-start job (`start-model`) claims only after its download; `download`,
`dig`, `add-list`, `spark-action`, `workflow-run`, `validate-workflows`, `self-test` and `undo` jobs take no claim (a
self-test’s steps do). One self-test runs at a time. It picks only a Spark nothing is changing on: no claim, and no
running operation that names it (a download-then-start holds no claim while it downloads); GPU apps there are read with
a fresh `docker ps`, never the watcher’s record.

## Why

Two starts would share memory and port.
