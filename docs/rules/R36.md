# R36

## Rule

**R36.** Apps on `/v1`: one place on Sparkie’s model stays for the owner’s chat. App calls to the brain at once are
capped at its `--max-running-requests` minus one (at least one; no cap when it is not set), over that 429 with
`Retry-After`; every way a call ends frees its place. The paste card (`running_models {client}`) is built by code from
the console’s setup: one generic card (a block only when `sparkie` is the brain itself), apps told at most 262,144
tokens of context beside the server’s own figure, a plain “not yet” for Codex and anything Claude, no key in or out,
withheld if masking would change it; Sparkie only hears it is shown, and the block is appended under its answer
exactly once; with another model chosen for agents there is no block, and Sparkie relays the plain values.

## Why

Apps must never crowd the owner out of their own brain, and the model must not garble an address.
