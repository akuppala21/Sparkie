# R62

## Rule

**R62.** The box (design A 2.4; step A2): `run {on: "box"}` opens the caller's box, a throw-away container on a compute
Spark that reaches nothing of the Spark but its own folder. One a chat (outside a chat, one a caller: the owner's API,
each agent key), named `spark-console-box-<12 hex>` from that chat or caller, opened on first use.

- **Where**: a compute Spark that is on (`boxPlaces`), the caller's last one first; never Spark 2 or a Spark holding
  the brain (R1), checked again just before each open (`boxRefusesHere`). It wakes nothing: with none on, it says so
  and nothing opens. Who may: as for a look (`whoMayLook`): the owner, an agent whose key may also change things,
  never a saved workflow's step.
- **The one line** (`workBoxCommand`), every word of it code's; from the caller comes only which chat it is (hashed
  into the name): `docker run --detach --rm --pull never --name <name> --label spark-console.box=<name> --user <the
  owner's ids> --network none --ipc none --cap-drop ALL --security-opt no-new-privileges --read-only --memory 4g
  --memory-swap 4g --pids-limit 256 --cpus 4 --ulimit fsize=<1 GiB> --mount <its folder> at /work --workdir /work --env
  HOME=/work --env TMPDIR=/work --entrypoint sleep <image> 7200`. The image is `config.json` `work.images.python`,
  pinned by digest (none pinned: nothing opens), fetched once apart from the call. Its folder,
  `~/.local/share/spark-console/work/<name>` (0700), is made empty at each open; the folders of boxes that have ended
  go at the next open on that Spark.
- **The check** (`BOX_CHECK`) runs inside the new box before anything else does, and must find every wall: not root
  (also from a child), no capabilities, no new privileges, nothing writable outside `/work`, `/work` writable, nothing
  mounted but its own folder at `/work` (and the three files Docker writes for every container), the owner's home not
  there, no socket at all (Docker's, containerd's, Tailscale's), no key handed in, no network device but loopback and
  no connection to the console or to Tailscale's resolver. Its lines begin with a random id; a wall missing, unknown
  or open removes the box, and the answer names what got through.
- **It ends** at its time (its `sleep` ends and `--rm` removes it), at `/stop` in its chat (`boxStop`: the container
  and its folder), and when its check fails. The watcher, and so `workloads`, leaves out a container whose
  `spark-console.box` label is its own name. Each open, refusal and removal is a journal record (`where: box`, R43).

## Why

One place where trying needs no yes and code from a web page may run, because it can reach nothing (design A 2.2 and
2.4; the one page, section 4, milestone 5); design C fills one with Spark Console's source (C8).

## Today

The command is not run: the answer says the box opened (or was open) and passed its check, and that nothing was run
in it; A4 runs commands and writes files there. The brain isn't told of the box yet: run's text and its list of Sparks
are as before. Which box is where lives in memory, so after a console restart a box left open ends at its own time,
and `/stop` no longer finds it. The check proves the walls on the day it runs; a real Spark's first check is the
owner's to see. The stand-in docker answers the check from the run's own flags.
