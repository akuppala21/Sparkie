# R25

## Rule

**R25.** A change from Sparkie runs at once only when the asker’s own words (this message, plus the previous user
message for the target when the asker wrote it: on the owner's turn one the owner typed, never an agent's, in any chat)
ask for that kind of change and name its target; else a button. Intent never comes from notices or tool results; which of
the owner's own words ask is R74 (pasted or quoted text asks nothing, a call-off wins, a change word asks only where an
order puts it). The trade: a statement and an order in one sentence (“The loader is broken, fix it.”) is a button; as
two sentences it runs. Where a change lands is the owner's to say (A-20), in this message only (`sparkPlaces`, R74):
one Spark named as a place and nothing else is where a start runs, whatever Spark the call names; none named, Spark
Console places it; several starts or keeps each take the place their own words name; anything else (one order naming
two, "3 or 4", one ruled out or not resolved, a stop or restart too) is a button showing the place the words settle. A
stop or restart runs at once on a Spark this message names and its words settle, or, none named, only if no
other Spark has a console container sharing a word with them. The audit line keeps the call's arguments as given, and in
`ranOn` the Sparks it ran on (none when nothing ran). Today: a Spark ruled out in an earlier message is not remembered.
On the owner's turn an agent's message goes to the brain labelled as the agent's, data
(`turnHistory`). Today the target check covers only arguments called `model`, `container`, `spark` and
`sparks`: for `cancel_operation`, `forget_workflow`, `test_changes` and `undo_update` the kind word alone is enough
(`run_workflow` has its own check, R35; `save_file` has its own, R37; `keep_running` has `appNamed`, R38; `run` and
`write_file` have the ladder’s, R61, where a go-ahead or a standing entry stands in for the words, R65).
`save_workflow` has its own check (F20): the steps are Sparkie’s writing, so it runs at once only when the turn read
nothing from outside (`ctx.readOutside`), the name is not saved yet, and every step’s model, container and Sparks are
in the owner’s words (`tracesToRequest` per step, on `stepArgs`); otherwise it is one button whose label lists the
steps as the console built them from the arguments (`pageOnlyLabel`, `stepWords`) and says when it replaces a saved
one. A change missing from `ACTION_KIND`
is always a button. `forget_memory` has its own check (A-3): it runs at once only when the line exists, this message
says to forget in so many words (“forget that”, “forget this”, “forget what I said”, “forget the note”, in a sentence
that is not a question, not called off, not after if, whether or should, and not in double quotes; only “can you” and
“could you” ask politely, “will you” and “would you” ask a question), no forget phrase anywhere in the message follows
don’t or never, and no later sentence only calls it off (“Actually don’t.”, “Never mind.”, “No wait, keep it.”), all
in `saysForget`; and the line is what they are talking about (0.3 of its words from this message or the one before,
never skipped); else the button that shows the line. `remember` is a button whenever the message rules the remembering
itself out (`refusesRemember`): don’t, do not, never or no need to, then a remember word at most two words on (“do not
remember …”, “don’t note …”), or a remember word in a sentence that is called off or followed by one that is. A “not”
anywhere else is part of the line (“don’t use Spark 1 from now on” keeps it at once), and “don’t forget”, “never
forget” and “do not forget” ask to remember and still keep the line at once. A line with an end (`until`) is a
button unless the owner's words name a time (“until Friday”, a day, a date: `TIME_WORDS`, R45). To turn the
words into a day the brain is told today's weekday and date (Spark 2's clock) in one line of the turn's record, after
the fleet's notes (`memoryTodayLine`; never in the system prompt or a tool's text, so the lock's mark doesn't change
from day to day), and `remember` says the end back in words (“until Friday 20 November”, `memoryDayWords`). The same
words said again are already kept only with the same end; with another end, or none, the new line takes the old one's
place, as a fact said again does (R45). A line the
owner says outright in their own page chat, or the paired Telegram chat (marked there), is kept by code at the turn's
end when the brain called neither `remember` nor `forget_memory` (`keepSaid`, R45): only a sentence of this message
that starts with “remember that”, “remember:”, “remember …” (three words or more, not “when”, “to”, “the” and the
like), “note that”, “keep in mind that”, “make a note that”, “from now on” or “going forward”, and is no question,
not after a colon, not quoted, in a code block or a `>` line. Never when `refusesRemember` holds, the message held a
key or looks pasted (over 6 lines or 1,000 characters), for an agent's or a carry-on turn's words, or
while agents are paused. The brain eval's switch `said` turns it off. From the paired Telegram chat
`remember` and `forget_memory` run on the owner's own words as on the page, through these gates, each line or retirement
marked as said there (`tgOwnMemory`: via Sparkie, channel telegram, from the user paired now; never an agent's chat, a
press or an erase). A press on a memory button there, an erase for good and memory's history wait for the page (R32's
`PAGE_ONLY`; contract 4.10, 5.5). `self_test` has its own check: the owner’s
words asking for it (“run the self-test”, “test the Sparks”, “test Spark 3”: kind `selftest` in `CHANGE_WORDS`) are
enough, since what it runs is fixed by code; a `spark` stays only when the owner named it. From an agent (its own call,
its key, or `ask_sparkie`) it is an owner-only question inside the action, as a new image in `keep_running` is.
