# R71

## Rule

**R71.** The lock-out net, the console's half (A13b, `21-net.mjs`; on: `NET_ON`). Moved word for word from R60.

On a compute Spark a lock-out change is done only as root (R70): as the owner it changes nothing, so it is answered "set root", nothing asked (`netChange`). As root it is
the dangerous ask (R54) only when a sentence of the owner's current message tells it to change a way in there,
nothing read since (a read that returned, by its record: a call refused before it ran is none) (else refused, saying
how to ask: `netAsks`); never the message before, nor a carry-on turn's
(`netNowSaid`), and its line 2 is then "You asked for this."; what the net can't put back (`lasting`) is not done. Only the press runs it:
the Spark's claim, the ways in tried (a new SSH connection, the helper through sudo, Tailscale online), the guard on
record (`net.json`), the journal's start line, then `root --net`. After it they are tried again from outside: all
that worked still work, `keep`; one doesn't, `putback` at once; no answer, the Spark's own timer, claimed until then.
Each outcome is told once. Sparkie and agents never reach `keep` or `putback`; `/stop` never stops the timer;
`/putback` (page or paired chat) puts back the newest open or kept guard (a kept one stays). While one is open
there, a second is refused unasked. At start each open guard on record is put back (or found back or kept), said.

## Why

R60's Why: a change to SSH, the firewall, Tailscale or a sign-in can cut off every way the owner reaches a Spark. The
console's half decides who may ask for such a change, runs it only after the owner's press, and tells the owner what
became of it.

## Today

As R60's Today. What the owner's words ask is word lists (`netAsks`): the way-in word must be what the change verb
acts on, in the six words after it, before a comma, a joining word or a place word (on, in, of, from, at), and not
the start of another thing's name ("the shell script", `NET_NAMED`). So "set PasswordAuthentication no in
sshd_config on Spark 3" is refused (say "edit sshd_config on Spark 3 …"), and "set the hostname in config.yaml on
Spark 3" or a sentence quoting someone else still passes, to the dangerous ask. A miss is refused, never run.
