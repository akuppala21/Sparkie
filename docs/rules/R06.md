# R6

## Rule

**R6.** A passkey-gated change is in `mayNeedPasskey` (path) and `needsPasskey` (method and body), with entries in
`describeChange` and `bodyLimit`; removing an agent, pausing and switching the needs-me line or approvals from Telegram
off (R32) never need one. A chat button asked with `ownerOnly` needs the passkey on the page whatever the lists say
(its ask pressed in the paired Telegram chat counts instead, only within R32's approvals); asked of a direct call (no conversation
to show it: an agent’s tool call, `POST /actions`, a saved step), `question` hands back no choice ids, only how the
owner says yes. With no passkey registered the gate lets everything through.

## Why

One list alone gates nothing.
