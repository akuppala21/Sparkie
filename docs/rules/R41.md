# R41

## Rule

**R41.** Smart plugs and powering Sparks off (contract 9.22; the owner, 2026-11-13: “shut down sparks when not in use,
not spark 2”). Reading: `get_nodes` on the plug controller’s WebSocket every 15 s; a reading older than
`PLUG_STALE_MS` (90 s), or from a plug the controller says isn’t answering, shows as not answering with no numbers.
GPU power and wall power keep their own names (`gpuPowerWatts`, `wall`). Switching: only `switchPlug` sends
`device_command` (On/Off cluster 6, "on"/"off", asked once more as "On"/"Off" unless refused for access), only for a
plug `switchablePlug` allows, and it is done only when a fresh reading shows the plug as asked. The hub
(`brainSparks()`) and the console’s own Spark are never switched: their plug is read only when `plugs.json` marks it
`readOnly` (`deploy/plugs.sh` pair or name writes that only after lowering the controller to View on that plug and seeing it
refuse an "on"), `power_off` and `power_on` refuse the hub before anything else, and only the owner’s Tapo app
switches it.

Powering off (`power_off`, and the idle loop through it) changes nothing and is refused for a Spark without a
switchable plug; while anything runs there that Spark Console didn’t start, anything protected, a job not ended or an
app not kept; when `sudo -n` doesn’t allow the two commands of `deploy/power-setup.sh`; and when a Spark that answers
hasn’t proven its plug (a fresh reading: answering, on, 15 W or more). Each is checked in the action and again in the
job. An agent’s `power_off` and `power_settings` always wait for the owner’s button, which the agent can’t press
(`power_on` stays open to agents); a model that answers Sparkie stops only after the owner’s yes (`confirmRouted`).
Spark Console’s models and kept apps there stop first and are parked (R19); across two Sparks both parts stop, and the
other Spark stays on, claimed. The record is written before the shutdown; a record that can’t be written, anything new
after the stops, or a look that fails puts back what was parked, and nothing shuts down. The shutdown is sent only by
`shutDownSpark`, which refuses `brainSparks()` and any local Spark whoever calls it (`deploy/power-setup.sh` also
refuses the hub by address, name and machine id). The plug is cut only once the Spark stops answering SSH
(`answersNow`, a fresh command) and a fresh reading is under 15 W, after one more check that it doesn’t answer. A
Spark that still answers is never cut; watts that never drop leave a `problem` on the record (a needs-me line). A
Spark that doesn’t answer when asked is cut only when its plug reads under 15 W at every poll for `POWER_HOLD_MS` (1
minute, `holdLow`), the plug answering and the Spark silent: the controller’s values are cached, of unknown age.
Before the parking, a Spark going down gets the state's copy first if it changed (R23).

Turning on (`power_on`, or a start that needs it, `powerThen`): plug on when it is off; off, 10 s, on when the Spark
is shut down with the plug on (under 15 W, held as above, so a Spark booting from its own button is never cut);
nothing for one that draws power but doesn’t answer. A Spark seen answering again by itself is only taken back. A
failed power-on writes `problem` on the record. What was parked comes back once its Spark answers (`bringBackParked`,
as self-healing would); while changes are paused or automatic restarts are off it is only unparked (held). Every
round, an entry still parked whose Sparks all answer, none off on purpose and no power operation on them, is unparked
with a notice (`unparkStranded`). A start turns Sparks on first when it names one Spark Console powered off, is for a
parked model, its model is on no Spark that is on, or it can’t be placed on a Spark that is on without stopping
something (it is placed again once they are on). Naming none, it wakes one, chosen from the records alone (`wakeOne`,
D5; each record holds the model folders on that Spark's disk, `memTotal` and `diskFree`, from the look before it went
off): one whose record lists the model, with its linked partner when one Spark's memory can't hold it; for a model on no
Spark that is on, else one with no list; only when nothing that is on has room, else the first whose recorded disk and
memory hold it, a record with no numbers last. A Spark off with no record is never woken, and a start that wakes none
says why. A read never wakes one: `models_on_disk` and `where_it_fits` answer from the records, with since when
(`asleepView`). A call at `/v1` still never downloads or stops a model for room.

The idle loop (`powerTick`, every 60 s) waits while agents’ changes are paused, a validation runs, or any of
`start_model`, `start_model_across` and `restart_model` isn’t active (`fleet_status` names them). It powers off a
Spark other than the hub after the owner’s idle minutes (30 by default; 0 is off; `power_settings`) with: no operation
or claim there; only Spark Console’s own models running (a kept app keeps it on); GPU under 5% and load under 2; no
chat, app, `/v1`, model copy or app-door use (a copy of the state is not use); no request counted by the model
server’s own `/metrics` since the last round (`metricsFlags` turns these on for SGLang and llama.cpp); nobody signed in over SSH
from another machine and no connection on the model port. A Spark whose model gives no counter is never idle
(`servesHere`). Both Sparks of a pair go together. The first time it can act, the owner is told once
(`sayIdleActive`). In chat, `power_off` and `power_on` run at once only when the owner’s phrase switches Sparks by
name and names every Spark it would switch (“Spark 3’s model” says whose, never a power-off). The calls the next phase
builds on are at the end of 21-power.mjs: `power.state`, `power.wake` (never the hub; its reason from `WAKE_WHY`),
`power.wallW`, `power.wallWh`, `sparkNeeded` (every reason a Spark may not sleep), `keepAwake` and `doneWith`.

## Why

Cutting the hub’s power would take down the console, Sparkie and the plug controller with no way back from here
(lock-out); cutting a Spark that is up loses work and can damage its disk; a plug on the wrong Spark would cut the
wrong one.

## Today

- Shutdown requires saved parked state. Failure leaves hardware on and attempts restoration. Cancel during boot
  ends before workload handoff. Wake reuse requires full target coverage; partial overlap is refused.
- Stand-ins only: command names, cached-reading delay (10 s confirmation, `SPARK_CONSOLE_PLUG_CONFIRM_MS`), shutdown
  draw (under 15 W) and firmware Auto Boot need live checks on Spark 3 or 4 before relying on idle shutdown.
  The hold substitutes for an unused direct attribute read.
- The `/metrics` flags and counter names were read from the engines’ own source (2026-11-14), not from the images in
  use. TensorRT-LLM gives no counter, so a Spark serving it never goes idle.
- Known limits the owner accepted (2026-11-14): work in a detached shell (`nohup`, `tmux`) or a hand `docker build` or
  pull with no SSH session open, under 5% GPU and 2 of load; an app opened on its tailnet address, not through the app
  door; a Spark with a kept app never sleeps by itself (an asked `power_off` parks apps).
- Power follow-ups: `REVIEW-GUIDE.md` §6.
