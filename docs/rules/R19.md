# R19

## Rule

**R19.** Healing is bounded: two crash restarts an hour, two bring-backs in six hours, two brain restarts in six
hours, two brain starts by the keeper in six hours; no other model heals on the brain’s Spark or a claimed one. The
brain keeper (`keepBrain`, from `watchBrain`) starts Sparkie’s model only when `main.json` carries `leftDown`, Docker
has said stopped or gone for `HEAL_SETTLE_MS`, and no brain job, restart or claim is on its Spark. `leftDown` is
written by `switchMain` before it stops the model in use and by `startMain` before it removes the container; it is
cleared when the model answers, by the page’s Stop, and after the second try (then one notice naming
`/restart sparkie`). The keeper starts `MAIN`, the saved setup, never another, through a `main-start` job marked
`noStops`; `startMain` then refuses if anything would stop, copy or download. Without the mark (stopped or removed by
hand, or the owner’s Stop) nothing starts. One model per Spark is wanted at a time: `healPass` does not bring back an
entry that is down while a newer model on the list (a later `since`, not an app) shares one of its Sparks
(`displacedBy`). It stays down while that newer one is on the list, running or down, so when the newer one crashes it
is the one restarted, never the older one in its place (gate, 2026-11-13: the two healers raced for the Spark). The
owner’s own start of an entry already on the list moves its `since` to that start (`desiredAfter`, no revision), so it
is the newest again. Healing never acts on a look Docker didn’t answer: `healReason` returns nothing while any of the
entry’s Sparks has `dockerOk` false (no stop, no start, no try used; R21). Today: `bringBack`’s stop of a pair’s
leftover part goes straight through `run`, with no claim, job or `runAction`; the brain’s restart (`restartBrain()`,
called by `watchBrain` and by the owner’s `/restart sparkie`, R34) holds the claim on the brain’s Spark and one
in-flight flag but has no job or `runAction`; the word also uses it to start a stopped brain, which healing never
does; the keeper’s job holds the claim but has no `runAction` or workflow; a Docker that does not answer reads as a
missing container.

## Why

Repeating a failure makes it worse.
