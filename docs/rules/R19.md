# R19

## Rule

**R19.** Nothing heals against an intentional power-off (21-power.mjs): an entry marked `parked`, or on a Spark
`power.json` says Spark Console powered off, is skipped by `healPass`, `healReason`, `healApp` and `maybeHeal`, shown
as parked (never held), and never makes a "should be running" notice or a needs-me line; it comes back when its Spark
is turned on (`power_on`, or seen answering again) or on its next call or start. An entry a saved workflow's clean-up
stopped and put back on the list (`putBack`, R57) is skipped by `healPass` and `healReason` the same way, until it next
runs. Healing is bounded: two crash
restarts an hour, two bring-backs in six hours, two brain restarts in six hours, two brain starts by the keeper in six
hours; no other model heals on the brain’s Spark or a claimed one. The brain keeper (`keepBrain`, from `watchBrain`)
starts Sparkie’s model only when `main.json` carries `leftDown`, Docker has said stopped or gone for `HEAL_SETTLE_MS`,
and no brain job, restart or claim is on its Spark. `leftDown` is written by `switchMain` before it stops the model in
use and by `startMain` before it removes the container; it is cleared when the model answers, by the page’s Stop, and
after the second try (then one notice naming `/restart sparkie`). The keeper starts `MAIN`, the saved setup, never
another, through a `main-start` job marked `noStops`; `startMain` then refuses if anything would stop, copy or
download. Without the mark (stopped or removed by hand, or the owner’s Stop) nothing starts. One model per Spark is
wanted at a time: `healPass` does not bring back an entry that is down while a newer model on the list (a later
`since`, not an app) shares one of its Sparks (`displacedBy`). It stays down while that newer one is on the list,
running or down, so when the newer one crashes it is the one restarted, never the older one in its place (gate,
2026-11-13: the two healers raced for the Spark). The owner’s own start of an entry already on the list moves its
`since` to that start (`desiredAfter`, no revision), so it is the newest again. Healing never acts while the status
check still has one of an entry’s Sparks off (`sparkOff`): until it records the power-on, what is known of power-ons
is the one before, which would skip the settle wait. Healing never acts on a look Docker didn’t answer: `healReason`
returns nothing while any of the entry’s Sparks has `dockerOk` false (no stop, no start, no try used; R21). Today:
`bringBack`’s stop of a pair’s leftover part goes straight through `run`, with no claim, job or `runAction`; the
brain’s restart (`restartBrain()`, called by `watchBrain` and by the owner’s `/restart sparkie`, R34) holds the claim
on the brain’s Spark and one in-flight flag but has no job or `runAction`; the word also uses it to start a stopped
brain, which healing never does; the keeper’s job holds the claim but has no `runAction` or workflow; a Docker that
does not answer reads as a missing container. The self-test’s healing drill (`healDrills`): the one container it
killed, by `<spark>/<id>`, counts as a crash in the watcher (exit 137 is otherwise someone’s stop; its `crashed` event
carries `drill: true`), and `maybeHeal` doesn’t apply the 10-minute rule to it. Every other rule holds (the owner’s
switch, two restarts an hour, offline in the last half hour, out of memory, the workflow’s state), and the drill asks
them before it kills (`healTriesThisHour`, `lastOnOff`, shared with `maybeHeal`, plus the owner’s switch and pause and
the restart workflow’s state): when healing wouldn’t act, nothing is killed and the line is “Healing: not tried”, with
why and when to ask again; “not tried” never fails the run. The heal’s restart is `restart_model`’s run, so it
validates that workflow when it waits for its first run. A drill that doesn’t come back marks the container stopped on
purpose (`consoleStopped`), so no late restart brings it back after the test. A planned sleep or wake (an event marked
`planned`: Spark Console's own power-off, or a wake since it went dark, `plannedWake`) is no outage: it doesn't count
as offline in the last half hour (`lastOnOff`), so a crash after a planned wake is restarted at once (D4).

## Why

Repeating a failure makes it worse.

## Today

Fresh installs explicitly opt in to brain startup; saved Stops stay stopped. Recovery does not spend attempts on a missing pinned image.
